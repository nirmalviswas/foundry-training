# ADR-001: Purchase Order as an intermediate entity between Supplier and Shipment

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Our shipment domain needed to model the relationship between suppliers and
the shipments that fulfill their orders. The question was whether Shipment
should link directly to Supplier, or whether Purchase Order should sit
between them as its own entity.

## Decision
We will model Purchase Order as a distinct entity, with Supplier ->
Purchase Order and Purchase Order -> Shipment as separate one-to-many
relationships. Shipment does not link directly to Supplier.

## Alternatives Considered
- Linking Shipment directly to Supplier (rejected — collapses the audit
  trail; we'd lose the ability to tell which authorized order a shipment
  fulfills, and couldn't represent partial shipments against one order).

## Consequences
Every shipment query that needs supplier info now requires a join through
Purchase Order rather than a direct lookup. This is a deliberate trade-off:
slightly more query complexity in exchange for a model that reflects how
orders and shipments actually relate in practice (a shipment can't exist
without an order authorizing it).
